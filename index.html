Our Shop

Sweet Creatures began as a home-based shop in 2020, during the pandemic. We named it after Harry Styles’ song “Sweet Creature.”

Today, our café and bakeshop in Malabon is a cozy place to gather over frappes, iced coffee, and nacho fries. We also make custom cakes for your celebrations.
