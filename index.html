Our Shop

Sweet Creatures started in 2020 as a home-based shop during the pandemic. It is named after the Harry Styles song Sweet Creature.

Today it is a cafe and bakeshop in Malabon where fans can hang out over frappes, iced coffee, and nacho fries, and where we make custom cakes for your celebrations.
