# Sweet Creatures Website

## Project Overview
This website was developed by BSIS/BSIT 3rd year students as part of their Web Development course. It is a company website for Sweet Creatures, a home-based cafe and bakeshop in Malabon that was founded in 2020. The site shows the shop's menu, custom cakes, photos, story, and contact details.

## Purpose
To give Sweet Creatures an online presence, show its menu and custom cake designs, and make it easier for customers to send inquiries and cake requests.

## Site Structure
- `index.html` - Homepage
- `menu.html` - Menu with categories and search
- `custom-cakes.html` - Custom cake information, request form, and policy
- `gallery.html` - Cake designs and shop photos
- `about.html` - Shop story, beliefs, and owner
- `contact.html` - Location, contact details, and message form
- `style.css` - Styling for all pages
- `script.js` - Interactivity for all pages
- `assets/` - Images, logo, and favicon

## Features
- Photo slider on the homepage
- Menu filter buttons and a search bar
- Custom cake request form with messages for empty or wrong fields
- Contact form with a message after sending
- Floating navigation bar that links to all six pages
- Layout that works on both mobile and desktop

## Design Concept
- **Color Scheme**: Forest green and cream for a natural, handmade bakeshop look
- **Typography**: Space Grotesk for headings and Inter for body text
- **Layout**: Same header, navigation bar, and footer on every page, with card grids and even spacing
