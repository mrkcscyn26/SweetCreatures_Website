Our Story

Sweet Creatures started with one oven, one recipe book, and a stubborn belief that dessert should make people grin.

Everything is baked in-house each morning — real butter, real chocolate, real fruit — and whatever doesn't sell today goes home with the staff, never into tomorrow's case.
